# Motiontest: Wawasan video production

This repository is separate from Nohkeyra/Pu, the real Wawasan app.

- The released APK defines every app screen; never fabricate buttons, statuses, or customer data.
- Screens for the new proof must be captured from the signed Android APK on a clean emulator.
- Film camera, masks, transitions and perspective are creative; pixels shown as app UI remain authentic.
- If an APK capture fails, stop rather than substituting a mock screen.
- Preserve the original Wawasan logo and wordmark.
- The old Remotion smoke-test composition is a technical test, not the new film concept.
- Review the encoded MP4 and actual frame grabs. Technical success is not artistic approval.
- Do not modify the original app or publish the commercial video without approval.
